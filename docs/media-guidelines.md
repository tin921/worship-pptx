# Background asset defaults

Use **1280 × 720 (720p), 16:9** for background images. The app uses static images
only; do not add animation files to its library or packages. Historical pilot
experiments remain outside the app payload. If video work is requested again,
720p remains the resolution preference.

Keep the upper half visually quiet. White Chinese lyrics suit darker backgrounds;
dark text suits daylight backgrounds. Users select the text colour in the app.
There is no shaded text panel. Check contrast in the lyric and credit area,
targeting at least 4.5:1, and prefer small image files.
