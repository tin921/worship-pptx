Let's build a Sunday worship presentation. Select a service on the left, then edit its name and date. Your work saves automatically. You can resize the panels, and the app remembers your window layout.

Begin with the invocation. Enter a title and paste the Scripture, keeping the performance order empty. The preview uses a reader at a lectern for untagged readings. This picture is a streaming layout guide, not a live camera feed.

Click the plus tab to add a song. Put V one and C in square brackets before the verse and chorus. Then enter the performance order, such as V one, C, V two, C. Copyright and song number belong in the two fields above.

Choose a Chinese font and adjust color, bold, and alignment. Auto finds a size that fits the text blocks and keeps updating as you edit. Choosing an explicit size turns Auto off. The song title slide has its own size, separate from the lyrics.

Open Edit to choose a background. The app matches the song against its local image library. The model starts loading in the background after launch; if necessary, this window waits for that same load. The counter shows images loaded so far, followed by the total ranked results.

There are five style filters: photography, illustration, abstract, three D, and minimalist. Toggle the styles to narrow the gallery. Scroll down for more images. These filters reuse the matching results; they do not run the same search for every picture.

Double-click a result to inspect the full image. Double-click again to return. You can also click the small expand box on a card. Escape closes that view. Opening the full image does not apply it to your slide.

For more choices, click Google image search. The browser opens real image results using a short English subject from the suggested theme. You can edit the keywords first. Check the image dimensions and usage rights before downloading a background.

Back in the picker, you can browse for a local image, paste an image, load a direct image URL, or drag a file into the window. Select the background you want, then click Apply.

The small background preview and the main preview control the same image. Scroll to zoom, then drag to pan. Use the plus and minus buttons for smaller changes. The camera guide stays fixed while the background moves.

Flip mirrors the background. The opacity slider fades it toward white, which can make dark lyrics easier to read. Reset restores the original zoom and position. Check the main preview as you make each adjustment.

Add the second song with verse and chorus tags and its performance order. Then add the third song. Each song keeps its own background and formatting. Finish with the Scripture reading after all three songs. Leave its performance order empty. You can drag tabs to change their order.

Press Escape to leave a text field before using navigation shortcuts. Left and right switch item tabs. Up and down move through that item's slides. Page Down selects the next service in the left sidebar. Page Up goes back. Navigation stops at each end, without wrapping around.

Press F five, or click Preview, to check the whole presentation. The footer shows the current item, its slide count, and the count for the entire deck. Press down to move ahead. Right works the same way. Press left to go back. Press Escape to return to editing.

Click Create to save a PowerPoint file. The suggested name uses the service date and language code, without a computer name prefix. Backgrounds and bundled fonts are included, while the slide text remains editable in PowerPoint.

Click Upload to save a local copy and send the presentation to the configured Google Drive folder. Check the filename and local folder. Editing this path saves it for next time. The checkbox controls whether matching filenames are replaced. Now start the upload and wait for confirmation.

Upload complete. The button is now disabled to prevent another upload. Open the folder link to check your file. The cloud copy is kept for ten days with this setup, while the local copy stays on your computer. Your presentation is ready for Sunday.
